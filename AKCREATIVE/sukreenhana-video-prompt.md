# sukreenhana.my: launch video prompt

## Page summary (source for the video)

**sukreenhana.my** is the official academic website of **Assoc. Prof. Dr Sukreen Hana binti Herman**, a materials scientist and electronics engineer at the **Microwave Research Institute (MRI), Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM)**.

- **Tagline:** "Sensing the world, *one thin film* at a time." (BM: "Mengesan dunia, *satu filem nipis* pada satu masa.")
- **What she does:** builds pH and ion sensors (EGFET/ISFET), memristive devices and nanostructured films (ZnO, TiO₂), and brings young Malaysians into science and technology.
- **Impact in numbers:** **185** publications (72 journal, 96 conference, 17 other; 123 indexed) · **50** research grants (18 as Principal Investigator, 4 international) · **32** postgraduate theses supervised (10 PhD, 19 Master's, 3 PhD ongoing) · **h-index 18** (Google Scholar) / 14 (Scopus) / 11 (WoS) · **17** industry consultations.
- **Six research areas:** Electrochemical & pH sensors · Memristive & memory devices · Thin films & nanostructures · Optical fibre & photonics · Energy & smart materials · STEM education & inclusion.
- **Timeline:** first publication 2001 (PZT thin films) → PhD in Materials Science 2009 → busiest year 2013 (27 publications) → first industry consultation 2017 (MDEC) → latest work in 2026 (EGFET pH sensing on flexible substrates).
- **Industry & community:** CelcomDigi, Maxis, MDEC, Maybank, MTDC, SSDU Innovations, including the *Future Skills Programme: AI & Robotics* (CelcomDigi, 2024) and the *Young Digital Innovators* programmes.
- **Site features:** dark "lab at night" look, animated molecular-lattice hero, arched glowing portrait, keyword marquee, colourful impact counters with animated meters, publication search across 185 works, EN | BM switch.
- **CTA:** "Let's collaborate" · sukreenhana.my

## Prompt (copy and paste)

```
You're a world-class motion designer building a launch video for sukreenhana.my, the academic website of Assoc. Prof. Dr Sukreen Hana binti Herman, materials scientist and electronics engineer at the Microwave Research Institute, Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), Malaysia.

Research and land the core value and narrative: she engineers thin films and sensors that read the world (pH, nitrate, heavy-metal ions in soil and water), and she brings young Malaysians into science and technology. Core line: "Sensing the world, one thin film at a time."

Scenes should be dynamic and confident: hook in the first second, then distinct beats for research proof, impact, and AI & future skills. Brand-accurate UI, type, color and motion. Score the music so every cut, animation hit and scene change lands on a beat or drop; retime until synced. Social energy, quick cuts.

FORMAT
- 30 seconds, 9:16 vertical (also deliver 16:9). Track around 120 BPM, modern electronic with a clear drop at 0:08 and a lift at 0:22. Cut on every beat or every 2 beats; big hits on the downbeat.

BRAND (use exactly)
- Background deep ink #07090f with soft teal/violet radial glows and subtle film grain.
- Main gradient: teal #2fd6c3 → blue #6aa8ff → violet #8b7cf6. Secondary accents: gold #e7b75f, rose #f2708a, mint #74e6a8.
- Headlines in Instrument Serif, with key words in italic gradient. UI and labels in Inter Tight.
- Motifs: drifting molecular lattice (glowing dots joined by thin lines), arched portrait frame with a glowing gradient border, frosted-glass stat cards, animated bar meters, italic keyword marquee, rotating multicolour border on the CTA button.

SCENES (beat-synced)
1. 0:00–0:02 HOOK: black screen, then a single teal dot pulses on the first beat and explodes into a molecular lattice. Text slams in, word by word on beats: "Sensing the world," then italic gradient "one thin film" and "at a time."
2. 0:02–0:05 WHO: the arched portrait frame draws on with its gradient border and the portrait fades in. Glass cards pop on beats: "185 publications" and "h-index 18". Lower third: "Assoc. Prof. Dr Sukreen Hana binti Herman · UiTM".
3. 0:05–0:08 RESEARCH PROOF: fast keyword marquee whips across, with one keyword per beat: "EGFET pH sensors", "ZnO nanorods", "TiO₂ thin films", "Memristive devices", "Fibre-optic sensing". Macro-style abstract visuals of thin-film layers stacking and nanorods growing.
4. 0:08 DROP, IMPACT: bento grid of colour-coded tiles slams in on the drop. Numbers count up with glow, and meters fill on each hit: 185 publications (teal), 50 research grants (gold), 32 postgraduate theses (mint), 17 industry consultations (rose), h-index bars 18 / 14 / 11 (blue).
5. 0:12–0:16 SIX THREADS: six research-area cards flip in one per beat, each with its icon and colour: Electrochemical & pH sensors · Memristive devices · Thin films & nanostructures · Optical fibre & photonics · Energy & smart materials · STEM education & inclusion. Headline: "Six threads, one question: how do materials sense?"
6. 0:16–0:19 TIMELINE: a glowing line races across the years 2001 → 2009 → 2013 → 2017 → 2026, with gradient bars of publications per year growing upward on beats.
7. 0:19–0:23 AI & FUTURE SKILLS: partner wordmarks hit on beats (CelcomDigi, Maxis, MDEC, Maybank). Feature "Future Skills Programme: AI & Robotics" and "Young Digital Innovators". Visual: lattice lines turning into a circuit, then a robot-arm silhouette, then students' hands at laptops (abstract, no identifiable faces). Text: "Taking science outside the lab."
8. 0:23–0:27 PRODUCT UI: quick cuts of the real site: hero, the EN | BM switch toggling, publication search typing "ZnO" with highlighted results, the impact meters filling.
9. 0:27–0:30 CTA: the "Let's collaborate" button with its rotating multicolour border pulses on the final beats. End card: "sukreenhana.my", plus the SH monogram in a gradient circle. Final hit on the last downbeat, then silence.

RULES
- Use only the facts above. Do not invent awards, quotes, prices or student names.
- Keep motion premium and precise (ease-out cubic, quick scale-ins, glow pulses). No cheesy transitions.
- Make text legible at phone size, with high contrast (white or light text on the dark background).
- Optional BM version: swap the on-screen text for "Mengesan dunia, satu filem nipis pada satu masa.", "Mari bekerjasama", and BM labels (penerbitan, geran penyelidikan, tesis pascasiswazah, perundingan industri).
```
