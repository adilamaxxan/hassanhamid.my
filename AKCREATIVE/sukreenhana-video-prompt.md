# sukreenhana.my: launch video prompt

## Page summary (source for the video)

**sukreenhana.my** is the official academic website of **Assoc. Prof. Dr Sukreen Hana binti Herman**, a materials scientist and electronics engineer at the **Microwave Research Institute (MRI), Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM)**.

- **Tagline:** "Sensing the world, *one thin film* at a time." (BM: "Mengesan dunia, *satu filem nipis* pada satu masa.")
- **What she does:** builds pH and ion sensors (EGFET/ISFET), memristive devices and nanostructured films (ZnO, TiO₂), and brings young Malaysians into science and technology.
- **Impact in numbers:** **185** publications (72 journal, 96 conference, 17 other; 123 indexed) · **50** research grants (18 as Principal Investigator, 4 international) · **32** postgraduate theses supervised (10 PhD, 19 Master's, 3 PhD ongoing) · **h-index 18** (Google Scholar) / 14 (Scopus) / 11 (WoS) · **17** industry consultations.
- **Six research areas:** Electrochemical & pH sensors · Memristive & memory devices · Thin films & nanostructures · Optical fibre & photonics · Energy & smart materials · STEM education & inclusion.
- **Timeline:** first publication 2001 (PZT thin films) → PhD in Materials Science 2009 → busiest year 2013 (27 publications) → first industry consultation 2017 (MDEC) → latest work in 2026 (EGFET pH sensing on flexible substrates).
- **Industry & community:** CelcomDigi, Maxis, MDEC, Maybank, MTDC, SSDU Innovations, including the *Future Skills Programme: AI & Robotics* (CelcomDigi, 2024) and the *Young Digital Innovators* programmes.
- **Site features:** dark "lab at night" look, animated molecular-lattice hero, arched glowing portrait, keyword marquee, colourful impact counters with animated meters, publication search across 185 works, EN | BM switch.
- **CTA:** "Let's collaborate" · sukreenhana.my

## Prompt (copy and paste): 15 seconds, English

```
You're a world-class motion designer building a 15-second launch video for sukreenhana.my, the academic website of Assoc. Prof. Dr Sukreen Hana binti Herman, materials scientist and electronics engineer at the Microwave Research Institute, Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), Malaysia.

Research and land the core value and narrative: she engineers thin films and sensors that read the world, and she brings young Malaysians into science and technology. Core line: "Sensing the world, one thin film at a time."

Scenes should be dynamic and confident: hook in the first second, then distinct beats for research proof, impact, and AI & future skills. Brand-accurate UI, type, color and motion. Score the music so every cut, animation hit and scene change lands on a beat or drop; retime until synced. Social energy, quick cuts.

FORMAT
- 15 seconds, 9:16 vertical (also deliver 16:9). English only.
- Track at 128 BPM, punchy modern electronic: build for 0:00–0:04, drop at 0:04, lift at 0:11, final hit at 0:14.5. Cut on every beat; big hits on the downbeat.

BRAND (use exactly)
- Background deep ink #07090f with soft teal/violet radial glows and subtle film grain.
- Main gradient: teal #2fd6c3 → blue #6aa8ff → violet #8b7cf6. Accents: gold #e7b75f, rose #f2708a, mint #74e6a8.

SCENES (beat-synced)
```
